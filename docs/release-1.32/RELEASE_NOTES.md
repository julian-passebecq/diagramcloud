# DiagramCloud 1.32.0: unified continuation

A minor release on top of [1.31.0](../release-1.31/RELEASE_NOTES.md). Document schema stays 1 and the model is unchanged. 1.32.0 lands the F/G/H source contribution (linked reading, domain correspondence, join inspection, meeting summary, review workspace and capacity arithmetic modules with their examples and contracts) and three integration lanes on top of it: linked views in the App (A), delivery evidence, history questions and join evidence (B), and the reconciled practical catalogue with the meeting summary form (C). The status of each acceptance row, with the test behind it, is in [V3_WORKSPACE](../product/V3_WORKSPACE.md) (UX26, UX27, UX29, UX30 updated; UX35-UX38 added). The boundaries are in AGENTS.md, "Current limits (1.32.0)".

## What changed

- **Linked views (UX36).** The V3 workspace has a "Linked views" toggle that shows two permitted views of the open project side by side, each with its own camera. A pick resolves by exact stable ID (same cut) or through explicit mapping tables (columns equal to the domain mapping contract, attached to their from-node); related is shown as "Related, not the same object", several targets are offered and never chosen, an unresolved mapping is a visible gap. The business anchor stays across swaps and view changes. One detail slot moves between below both views and below the right view; its placement and split are saved only by "Save linked layout" (`diagramcloud.linked-layout.v1:<scope>`). The pair and anchor are route state (`linked=<left>~<right>`, `anchor=`), so Back and Forward restore them. At 390 x 844 one pane shows at a time. Portfolio and Present read publicDocument.
- **Default surface.** "Open the V3 workspace by default" (Layout details) stores `diagramcloud.default-surface.v1` per host; `?workspace=studio` always opens the classic studio; a corrupt or unavailable value falls back to the studio. Nobody's default is flipped.
- **Delivery plan (UX26, UX27, UX29, UX30).** The workspace dock "Plan, owners and remaining effort" reads a supplied `delivery-view/1` file (at most 256 KiB, 200 blocks), session-only and never written into the Project: source identity with the input sha256, blocks with source-declared states, owner conflicts (unordered overlap or sequential), named gaps, uncalibrated remaining ranges with a dependency lower bound and no date, supplied intervals and result references with their basis. A private plan is withheld from Portfolio and Present. There is no dispatch, launch, approve or edit control.
- **History questions (UX30).** The dock "History questions" answers ancestry, associations, attempts, impact and lineage over the atlas slice or a session-loaded history slice; impact matches exact component IDs only, associations never become edges, a question without facts names the missing facet.
- **Join evidence (UX37).** The selection evidence shows the join-inspection blocks of a component (input and output rows, measure before and after, fanout, unmatched rows, grain violations, per-row provenance, limits; inconsistent blocks flagged, not repaired). In Edit mode, "Inspect a join" in the workspace explorer reads a join request, calculates over its rows (no SQL) and stages six private blocks as one revision-guarded patch through the ordinary review.
- **Meeting summary (UX38).** Export & share has "Meeting summary (HTML)": 1-6 public reachable views, design or business profile, A4 or letter, optional story stops; offline HTML with no script; print to PDF from the browser.
- **Catalogue.** 12 practical cards join the 12 R4 cards (24 lessons, 36 catalogue rows, 43 sources); two candidates were dropped as duplicates ([CATALOGUE-RETURN](../product/CATALOGUE-RETURN.md)). The legacy matcher snapshot is unchanged. Practical alternatives open as "Candidate, not selected."
- **Fix found by the local check.** A real Galaxy plan (checked locally, never committed) was refused whole because two descriptive fields the reading never interprets were a small record and a list of numbers. Descriptive fields now accept any bounded JSON value (depth 4, 200 items, 8000 characters per string) and stay unread; `tests/delivery-evidence.test.ts` "delivery plan: descriptive fields of any bounded JSON shape are accepted and never read" covers it with a synthetic plan.
- **Release journeys.** `tests/e2e/v3-release.spec.ts` adds three offline journeys through the real App: "Galaxy-style plan: source identity, owner conflict, named gaps, remaining range, intervals, results and five history questions", "Synthetic commerce: join 42 to 70, stage evidence, meeting summary" and "World/data correspondence with a catalogue pattern" (1440 and 390).

## Limits

- Linked views read one project: same-cut pairs or mapping tables in that project. Cross-project correspondence stays the recorded-correspondence comparison. The pair is not a saved-context dimension. No free docking.
- The delivery plan is a reading of supplied text: no token forecast, no calendar, no queue or access model, no live collector, no link from a block to a PM task. Effort ranges are uncalibrated allowances.
- The meeting summary is HTML only; PDF is the browser's print, a snapshot, not a backup; no PPTX. No person opened a printed PDF for this release.
- `capacityScenario` has no App surface. New lessons have no semantic diagrams. The VS Code / Cursor hosts are unchanged and their installed runs were not repeated.
- Real Galaxy plans and FOIL projects were checked locally only (validation and public projection), never committed.
- Only Chromium is qualified.
